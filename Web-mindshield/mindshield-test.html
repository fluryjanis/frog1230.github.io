🔬 The Science of Cognitive Offloading

When we immediately turn to AI for every question, our brains engage in cognitive offloading—the psychological tendency to reduce mental effort by relying on external tools.

📉 The Atrophy of Problem Solving

Peer-reviewed research in cognitive psychology shows that bypassing the friction of independent problem-solving weakens working memory retention and reduces structural endurance over time.

⚡ The Generation Effect

Formulating your own hypothesis—even for just 5 seconds before receiving an answer—activates the Generation Effect, strengthening long-term neural connections.
